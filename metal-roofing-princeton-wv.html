Metal roofing FAQ

Is metal roofing a good choice for homes in Princeton, WV?

Metal roofing is often a strong choice for Princeton homes because it offers durability, strong weather resistance, and a long service life compared with many traditional roofing systems.

What is the difference between standing seam and exposed fastener metal roofing?

Standing seam metal roofing uses concealed fasteners and has a cleaner finished appearance, while exposed fastener systems can be more budget-friendly but differ in long-term maintenance and design.

How long does a metal roof last?

Many metal roofing systems are selected for their long-term performance and can outlast standard asphalt roofing by a significant margin when installed correctly.
